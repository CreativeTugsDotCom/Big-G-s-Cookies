Our Heritage

The Story Behind Big G's Cookies

Big G's Cookies started with a simple home kitchen dream: to craft honest, delicious cookies that bring a little extra sweetness into everyday life. Based in Trumbull, Connecticut, our bakery operates with a deep commitment to home-baked quality and community connection.

We believe that the best cookies aren't manufactured on massive conveyor belts. They come from careful ingredient selection, small batches, and a baker who genuinely cares about every tray coming out of the oven.

"Baking is our way of sharing happiness with our neighbors—one tray of cookies at a time."
